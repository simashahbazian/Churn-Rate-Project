 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

 🔎 Overview

This project presents an independently developed customer churn analysis
using Microsoft Excel for data preprocessing and Power BI for data
modeling, analytical measure development, visualization, and interactive
analysis.

The objective is to identify patterns associated with customer churn and
explore how churn varies across demographic, contractual, behavioral, and
service-related characteristics.

The analysis also examines churn reasons and their associated revenue
impact.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies   

 📗 **Microsoft Excel** — Data preprocessing and cleaning
 📊 **Power BI** — Data modeling, analysis, and interactive visualization
 🧮 **DAX** — Analytical measures and KPI development
 🔄 **Power Query** — Data transformation and preparation
 🎛️ **Power BI Slicers & Cross-filtering** — Interactive data exploration

 📈 Dashboard Structure

The dashboard is organized into three analytical pages, each focusing on a
different aspect of customer churn.

 1️⃣ Overview

Provides a high-level view of the customer base and overall churn behavior.

Key metrics and analyses include:

 👥 Total Customers
 🔴 Churned Customers
 📉 Churn Rate
 💰 Total Revenue
 📞 Customer Service Calls
 📊 Average Customer Service Calls
 🎂 Churn rate by age group
 🗺️ Churn rate by state
 💵 Revenue by contract type
 📶 Churn by data consumption and unlimited data plans
 🏷️ Churn categories

 2️⃣ Churn Risk Segmentation

This page focuses on understanding how churn varies across different
customer segments.

The analysis includes:
 👤 Account length
 👥 Customer demographics
 📄 Contract type
 ⚧️ Gender
 🗺️ Geographic distribution
 💬 Churn reasons
 📞 Customer service interactions

Interactive filters allow different customer segments to be explored
across the analytical views.

 3️⃣ Churn Root Cause

This page investigates the main reasons associated with customer churn
and their financial impact.

The analysis includes:

 🔍 Churn reasons
 🏷️ Churn categories
 📞 Customer service interactions
 💳 Average monthly charges
 💰 Extra charges
 📉 Lost Monthly Revenue (MRR)
 💵 Revenue impact by churn reason

The objective is to move beyond measuring churn rates and examine the
patterns and financial impact associated with customer churn.

📌 Key Metrics

The dashboard includes analytical measures for monitoring customer churn
and its business impact, including:

 👥 Total Customers
 🔴 Churned Customers
 📉 Churn Rate
 🔄 Retention Rate
 💵 ARPU (Average Revenue per User)
 💰 Total Revenue
 📉 Lost Monthly Revenue (MRR Lost)
 📞 Average Customer Service Calls
 💳 Average Monthly Charge
 💰 Average Extra Charges
 ⚠️ High-Impact Reasons Count
 📊 Contract- and plan-specific churn metrics

 🎛️ Interactive Analysis

The dashboard is fully interactive and supports slicer-based exploration
and cross-filtering across multiple dimensions.

Available filters include:

 🏷️ Churn Category
 💬 Churn Reason
 📄 Contract Category
 🗺️ State
 🎂 Age
 👤 Customer ID
 🔴 Churn Label

Users can select specific customer segments and dynamically explore how
the selected filters affect the different analytical views.

 💡 Key Findings

The analysis identified differences in churn rates across customer
segments, contract types, service interaction levels, and churn reasons.

The dashboard also highlights the financial impact associated with
customer churn through metrics such as Lost Monthly Revenue (MRR).

> ⚠️ The findings describe patterns observed in the analyzed dataset and
> are not intended to establish causal relationships.
